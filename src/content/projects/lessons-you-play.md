I am tired of explaining a Bloom filter on a whiteboard and watching everyone nod and forget it by lunch. So each lesson here teaches one mechanism as a small hand drawn world that you click through a step at a time. A Bloom filter is a seed bed with twelve holes and consistent hashing is fish dealt to boats round a lagoon. If your favourite mechanism is missing you can ask for it and a routine reads the requests every hour and builds one.
