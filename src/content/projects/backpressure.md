I am tired of opening Excalidraw to design around client requirements that violate the laws of physics and the CAP theorem in the same sentence. So I turned the requirements call into a Cities Skylines style game. A client hands you ten numbers and one budget and a casual last line that turns out to matter. You build the AWS city that is supposed to survive it and then you press play and let the traffic review your architecture.
