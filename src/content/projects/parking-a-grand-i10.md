My wife has one complaint about my driving which is that I park too far from everything. Too far from the wall and too far from the footpath and far enough from the car in front that a second car fits in the gap. The reasonable fix was to practise in the actual car so I built a simulator of our Grand i10 instead. Park it and step out and it tells you in centimetres how right she was. Bring a keyboard.
